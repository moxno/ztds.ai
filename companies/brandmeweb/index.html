Organizational Overview & Mandate

BrandMeWeb is an international SEO and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) agency founded by Ilya Sibiryakov. The agency engineers machine-readable corpus architecture, Linked Data Knowledge Graphs, and high-authority search visibility for digital brands, enterprise SaaS, and regulated verticals.

As a Founding Corporate Member of the ZTDS AI Consortium, BrandMeWeb strictly enforces client-side zero-server data sanitization across 100% of its internal AI prompt engineering, client analytics, and competitive intelligence workflows.

Certified Compliance Scope

  • Client Analytics Protection: Client domain names, revenue metrics, conversion goals, and private keyword corpora are masked in local RAM prior to LLM evaluation.
