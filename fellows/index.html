Consortium Working Groups (WGs)

Specialized research councils driving cryptographic rigor, legal enforceability, and edge systems engineering.

Consortium Governance Charter →

WG-1: Cryptography & Entropy

Formalizes deterministic reversible tokenization algorithms, pseudo-random surrogate generation, and differential privacy guarantees. Proves mathematical non-recoverability of masked sensitive fields under adversarial model probing.

WG-2: GRC & Legal Defense

Authors statutory defense frameworks under GDPR Recital 26 (anonymous data exemption), HIPAA Safe Harbor (Section 164.514(b)), EU AI Act Article 50, and US FRE 502 statutory attorney-client privilege.

WG-3: Systems & WASM Runtime

Maintains the sub-2ms WebAssembly zero-trust execution engines, proxy-wasm filter specifications for Envoy/Istio service meshes, stdio Model Context Protocol (MCP) daemons, and AWS Nitro hardware enclave attestations.
