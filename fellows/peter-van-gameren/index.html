Professional Profile & Contributions

Peter van Gameren is the author of Artificial Responsiveness: Clarity Before Scale in the Age of AI and Managing Consultant at Match2Market, a commercial strategy practice in the Netherlands. He works with leadership teams to align commercial architecture, customer operations, and AI adoption.

As an Industry Fellow for the ZTDS AI Consortium, he conducted an independent 90-minute empirical validation of in-browser client-side sanitization. His testing calibrated Dutch Burgerservicenummer (BSN) recognition, European initial-prefixed surname patterns, and in-chat DOM prompt interception under GDPR Article 9 & 28 standards.
