Direct Answer Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does ZTDS GitOps accreditation and verification take? ↓
Automated CTS invariant verification via `npx ztds-audit` completes locally in under 60 seconds. Once a GitOps Pull Request or attestation payload is submitted with a valid SHA-256 compliance receipt, the Consortium Review Board merges verified entries into the public registry within 48 hours.
What are the three accreditation tracks in the ZTDS ecosystem? ↓
Track A certifies software products, SDKs, and MCP servers meeting the four RFC v1.0 invariants; Track B accredits Corporate Adopters enforcing client-side sanitization across internal AI workflows; and Track C inducts Research Fellows contributing peer-reviewed cryptography, GRC legal treatises, or runtime engineering.
