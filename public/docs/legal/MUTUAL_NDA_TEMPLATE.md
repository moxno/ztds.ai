# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (M-NDA)

**PRE-SALES TECHNICAL & COMMERCIAL EVALUATION AGREEMENT**

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into as of [Date] ("Effective Date"), by and between:

**PARTY A:** Ilya Sibiryakov / BrandMeWeb ecosystem ("Licensor"), and  
**PARTY B:** [Company Name], a corporation organized under the laws of [Jurisdiction], with principal offices at [Address] ("Company").

Licensor and Company may each be referred to individually as a "Party" or collectively as the "Parties."

---

## 1. Purpose of Disclosure
The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship concerning the licensing, evaluation, and architectural integration of the Zero-Trust Data Sanitization (ZTDS) software engine and enterprise AI security infrastructure ("Purpose").

---

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any proprietary, non-public, confidential, or sensitive information disclosed by one Party ("Disclosing Party") to the other Party ("Receiving Party"), whether orally, visually, in writing, electronically, or in machine-readable form.

* **Licensor Confidential Information includes:** Software architecture, source code, compiled WebAssembly modules, regex entity taxonomy profiles, pricing models, product roadmaps, and technical know-how.
* **Company Confidential Information includes:** Internal network topologies, cloud architecture, AI pipeline specifications, prompt structures, security posture assessments, compliance vulnerabilities, and evaluation criteria.
* **Exclusions:** Confidential Information does not include information that:
  (a) Is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement;
  (b) Was already in the rightful possession of the Receiving Party prior to disclosure without confidentiality restrictions;
  (c) Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or reliance on the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or
  (d) Is rightfully received from a third party without duty of confidentiality.

---

## 3. Obligations of Receiving Party
3.1. **Standard of Care:** The Receiving Party agrees to protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information with the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of like importance, but not less than reasonable care.

3.2. **Limited Use:** The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose described in Section 1.

3.3. **Limited Disclosure:** The Receiving Party shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party, except to its officers, directors, employees, contractors, and legal/financial advisors who have a strict need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as those contained herein.

3.4. **Legally Compelled Disclosure:** If the Receiving Party is legally compelled by court order, subpoena, or regulatory directive to disclose Confidential Information, it shall provide prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party (to the extent legally permissible) to enable the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order.

---

## 4. Proprietary Rights & No License
All Confidential Information remains the exclusive property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any license, patent right, copyright, trademark, or ownership interest in the other Party's Confidential Information or intellectual property.

---

## 5. Term & Return of Information
5.1. **Term:** This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of two (2) years from the Effective Date. The confidentiality obligations regarding trade secrets (including software algorithms, heuristics, and regex profile taxonomies) shall survive indefinitely until such trade secrets become publicly known through lawful means.

5.2. **Return or Destruction:** Upon written request of the Disclosing Party or upon termination of discussions, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all physical and electronic copies of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, provided that archival backup copies maintained in the ordinary course of business may be retained subject to ongoing confidentiality protections.

---

## 6. Governing Law & Remedies
6.1. **Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware (or the State of Israel for EMEA engagements), without regard to conflict of laws principles.

6.2. **Injunctive Relief:** The Parties acknowledge that unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information would cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate. Accordingly, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek equitable relief, including temporary restraining orders and preliminary injunctions, without the necessity of posting bond.
