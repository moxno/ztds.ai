## 6. E-FILING PROCEDURE GUIDE FOR CHIEF ARCHITECT

### 6.1 Trademark E-Filing Procedure (Madrid Protocol via ILPO)
1. Navigate to the ILPO Online Services Portal (`gov.il/he/departments/israel_patent_office`).
2. Log in using Government Identification Gateway (Personal Smart Card / ID).
3. Select **"הגשת בקשה בינלאומית לסימני מסחר (פרוטוקול מדריד)"** (International Trademark Application under Madrid Protocol).
4. Reference Basic Application Number: **#182655957**.
5. Upload completed **Form MM2** (English) and **Form MM18** (USPTO Intent to Use).
6. Verify Goods & Services in Classes 9 and 42 strictly match `WIPO_MADRID_TRADEMARK_FILING_DOSSIER.md`.
7. Pay ILPO national handling fee (~400 ILS).
8. Provide WIPO Current Account details or credit card payment authorization for International Bureau fees.
9. Archive official ILPO and WIPO confirmation receipts in `.agents/ip-records/`.

### 6.2 Patent E-Filing Procedure (PCT via ePCT)
1. Access WIPO ePCT Portal (`pct.wipo.int`) using two-factor authentication.
2. Create New International Application.
3. Select Receiving Office: **RO/IL** (Israel Patent Office) or **RO/IB** (International Bureau, Geneva).
4. Claim Priority from **IL 331905** (Filing date: 14/09/2026).
5. Enter WIPO DAS Access Code: **B17B**. This enables the International Bureau to automatically retrieve the certified copy of the priority application directly from the Israel Patent Office digital library without manual paper certification fees.
6. Upload Description, Claims (20 claims), Abstract, and Drawings in PDF format meeting WIPO Standard ST.25.
7. Designate International Searching Authority: **ISA/EP** (European Patent Office - recommended for highest international examination rigor) or **ISA/IL** (cost-optimized).
8. Submit electronically prior to midnight Jerusalem time on **September 14, 2027**.
