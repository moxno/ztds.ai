Frequently Asked Questions

How do regulated healthcare and legal enterprises deploy ZTDS in production? ↓
Regulated enterprises deploy ZTDS at the originating client or microservice boundary (via browser extension, MCP stdio server, or headless Node.js/WASM SDK) to tokenize HIPAA PHI, legal work product under FRE 502, and PCI-DSS financial records in volatile RAM before prompts reach frontier LLMs or vector databases.
Are the architectural reference blueprints on ZTDS.ai vendor-neutral? ↓
Yes. All architectural case studies and reference blueprints on ZTDS.ai validate conformance with the four open ZTDS RFC v1.0 invariants and can be implemented using the Apache-2.0 reference engines or certified commercial implementations such as PrivacyScrubber.
