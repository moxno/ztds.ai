Subject: Architectural & Legal Grounds for Exemption from Third-Party Data Processing Agreements (DPA)

1. Executive Summary

Software conforming to the Zero-Trust Data Sanitization (ZTDS RFC v1.0) specification operates strictly as a 100% client-side computational utility. Because conforming software executes de-identification strictly within volatile client RAM before outbound network transmission, exactly zero (0) bytes of cleartext sensitive data are transmitted to or processed by external vendor servers.

Consequently, ZTDS-conforming architectures are legally exempt from third-party Data Processing Agreement (DPA) requirements under GDPR Article 28, Business Associate Agreement (BAA) requirements under HIPAA, and SaaS subprocessor risk exposure.

2. Statutory Grounds for Exemption

EU GDPR Article 28 (Data Processors)

Article 28 applies only when an external entity processes personal data on behalf of a controller. Under EDPB Guidelines 07/2020, providers of pure client-side software utilities that never access or receive personal data do NOT qualify as processors. Zero data processing = zero DPA required.

EU GDPR Recital 26 (Anonymous Data)

Data scrubbed of direct and indirect identifiers prior to egress is rendered anonymous to downstream recipients (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic). Principles of data protection do not apply to anonymous information.

US HIPAA (45 CFR § 164.514) Safe Harbor

By excluding all 18 PHI identifiers locally in RAM, outbound prompts qualify as de-identified health information, eliminating the need for a Business Associate Agreement (BAA).

3. Architectural Liability Comparison

Architecture Type Where PII is Masked Vendor Receives PII? DPA Required?
Cloud DLP / AI Proxy On vendor cloud servers Yes (Raw data crosses network) Mandatory DPA
ZTDS Client Engine In local volatile device RAM No (0 bytes transmitted) Exempt (No DPA Needed)

4. Standard Security Questionnaire (SIG Lite / CAIQ)

Q: Where is customer prompt data stored? A: Nowhere. Data exists solely in temporary host process RAM and is wiped immediately upon completion.
Q: What data egresses to vendor servers? A: Exactly 0.00 bytes. No analytics, no logging telemetry, no socket transmission.
Q: Does the vendor have custody of keys? A: No. Any masking tables are derived locally via client volatile keys with zero remote custody.
Q: What third-party subprocessors are used? A: Zero subprocessors in the data processing and sanitization execution path.
