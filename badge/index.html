Direct Answer Knowledge Base

Trust Badge Generator & Dynamic Seal Foundry FAQ

How do ZTDS Verified SVG trust badges work in GitHub READMEs and enterprise footers? ↓
The Badge Foundry generates crisp, zero-JavaScript vector SVG seals (/badge/.svg and /api/badge/) that link directly to the entity cryptographic verification record in the ZTDS Registry, allowing prospective customers and auditors to inspect conformance in one click.
What badge tiers and themes are supported by the dynamic /api/badge/ endpoint? ↓
The dynamic badge endpoint supports five accreditation tiers (VERIFIED, SOVEREIGN, DPA EXEMPT, AIR-GAPPED, and SELF-ATTESTED), dual color themes (theme=dark and theme=light), and custom architectural labels such as WASM-ONLY and SOC-2-READY.
Does embedding a ZTDS trust badge introduce third-party tracking cookies or client scripts? ↓
No. All static and dynamic ZTDS badges are served as pure, stateless image/svg+xml assets with strict cache headers and zero cookies, scripts, or tracking beacons, preserving strict zero-telemetry compliance on host sites.
Who is authorized to display the official ZTDS Verified and Sovereign seals? ↓
Software builders, corporate adopters, research fellows, and protected web domains listed in data/registry.json, data/companies.json, data/fellows.json, or data/domains.json with a valid Ed25519 conformance certificate are authorized to embed official ZTDS seals across their documentation and web properties.
