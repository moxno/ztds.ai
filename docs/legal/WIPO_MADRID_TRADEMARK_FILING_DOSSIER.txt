## 5. ZTDS VERIFIED™ CERTIFICATION MARK REGULATIONS & USAGE GOVERNANCE

Pursuant to standard certification trademark regulations, the applicant maintains an open, non-discriminatory governance framework:

### 5.1 Standards of Certification
To qualify for authorized public display of the mark **"ZTDS"** or **"ZTDS VERIFIED™"**, the applicant's product, agent, or service must demonstrate strict, verifiable compliance with:
1. **Invariant 1:** 0.00 bytes of unsanitized cleartext transmitted across any network interface prior to sanitization.
2. **Invariant 2:** Reversible bracketed surrogate tokenization preserving transformer neural network attention syntax.
3. **Invariant 3:** Cryptographic execution isolation inside volatile memory with verified zeroization.
4. **Invariant 4:** Zero subprocessor network dependencies, ensuring vendor immunity from GDPR Article 28 liability.

### 5.2 Verification Methodology
Compliance is audited deterministically via:
- The automated CLI suite (`npx ztds-audit --cert --applicant <NAME> --product <PRODUCT>`);
- The Autonomous Certification Agent (`bin/ztds-agent.js` / GitOps PR engine); or
- An independent certified security audit by an accredited third-party testing laboratory.

### 5.3 Prohibited Uses & Revocation
- **False Claims:** Any entity falsely claiming "ZTDS Compliance" without an active, cryptographically signed Ed25519 certificate issued by the ZTDS AI Consortium will receive a formal cease-and-desist order.
- **Automatic Revocation:** In the event of an unpatched 0-day network egress vulnerability (Severity Critical), the entity's certificate and authorization to display the ZTDS mark are revoked within 24 hours under the ZTDS Conformity Assessment Policy.
