Consortium Governance & Charter

The ZTDS AI Consortium is an independent, vendor-neutral standard-setting organization dedicated to zero-trust data sanitization in generative AI and automated systems. Governed under open consensus, academic peer review, and irrevocable zero-royalty patent covenants.

1. Executive Charter & Architectural Mission

The primary purpose of the ZTDS AI Consortium is to establish, maintain, and verify provable industry standards that guarantee unmasked personally identifiable information (PII), protected health information (PHI), and intellectual secrets are mathematically incapable of crossing runtime execution perimeters.

PRINCIPLE 01

Architectural Neutrality

The ZTDS standard operates independent of specific cloud providers, AI model vendors, or SaaS intermediaries. Specifications are formal mathematical contracts, not proprietary product lock-ins.

PRINCIPLE 02

Empirical Reproducibility

Every specification requirement MUST be auditable via verifiable network telemetry interception, cryptographic checksum validation, or reproducible unit benchmarks.

PRINCIPLE 03

Public Domain Integrity

All normative specification documents are published under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC-BY-4.0) with immutable DOI anchors registered via Zenodo, OSF, and CERN registries.

2. Active Working Groups (WGs)

Consortium activities are partitioned into three specialized working groups. Each working group is co-chaired by an accredited ZTDS Fellow and an industry member representative.

WG-1

Architecture & Cryptography

Scope: Invariants 1 & 3 · RFC Core

Responsible for the mathematical proof definitions of Invariant 1 (Zero External Egress) and Invariant 3 (Hardware Enclave & WASM Isolation). Maintains formal schemas for deterministic tokenization matrices, cryptographic attestation manifests, and AES-256-GCM / Ed25519 audit receipts.

Nitro Enclaves Attestation WASM Sandbox Limits RFC Proof Validation
WG-2

GRC & Regulatory Compliance

Scope: Invariant 4 · Legal Defense

Maintains cross-jurisdictional compliance mappings between ZTDS invariants and global privacy statutes: GDPR Recital 26 (anonymous data exemption from DPA obligations), EU AI Act Article 10 (data governance without PII ingestion), HIPAA Safe Harbor 45 CFR §164.514, and US Federal Rule of Evidence 502(b).

Zero-DPA Guidance EU AI Act Harmonization CISO SIG Lite & CAIQ Mappings
WG-3

Developer Ecosystem & Tooling

Scope: Invariant 2 · Reference Implementations

Standardizes polyglot client-side engines and Model Context Protocol (MCP) stdio interfaces. Manages the official verification test harnesses (`npx ztds-audit`), benchmark runner suites, and GitOps CI certification pipelines.

@privacyscrubber/sdk Integration Cursor & Claude MCP stdio CLI Network Inspector

3. RFC Specification Lifecycle

All changes to the ZTDS technical specification progress through five formal lifecycle states modeled after IETF RFC procedures, requiring peer consensus and empirical proof.

STAGE 01
Working Draft
RFC-WD

Initial document drafted by an accredited Fellow or Working Group member. Open for community comment.

STAGE 02
Proposed Standard
RFC-PS

Formal review period (min. 60 days). Requires mathematical proof or formal verification model.

STAGE 03
Candidate Standard
RFC-CS

Requires at least two independent, interoperable implementations demonstrating compliance in production.

STAGE 04
Ratified Standard
RFC-RS (Active)

Approved by 2/3 supermajority of the Steering Committee. Assigned immutable DOI and published in registry.

STAGE 05
Deprecated
RFC-HIST

Superseded by newer specifications or retired due to cryptographic consensus changes.

4. RAND-Z Patent Covenant & IP Policy

To protect enterprise adopters and developers from predatory patent assertion, all contributors to the ZTDS standard operate under an irrevocable worldwide zero-royalty patent covenant.

Royalty-Free Perpetual Grant (RAND-Z)

Every participating member, corporate sponsor, and fellow irrevocably grants to any implementer of a ratified ZTDS standard a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual patent license covering any essential patent claims required to practice the standard.

Defensive Patent Retaliation Clause

If any party initiates patent infringement litigation against a certified ZTDS implementer claiming that standard-compliant software infringes their patents, all patent licenses granted to that party under this charter terminate automatically with retroactive effect.
