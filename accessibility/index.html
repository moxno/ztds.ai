Technical Accessibility Statement

ZTDS.ai is engineered in accordance with W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.2 Level AA) and Israeli Standard IS 5568 technical accessibility guidelines so that security engineers, researchers, and legal counsel using assistive technologies can inspect specifications and cryptographic verification tools without barriers.

01. Implemented Technical Accessibility Controls

Rather than injecting intrusive third-party JavaScript overlay widgets that compromise client privacy or violate CSP Level 3 boundaries, accessibility controls are built natively into the semantic HTML5 and CSS architecture of ZTDS.ai:

Control 01

Keyboard Navigation & Skip Landmarks

Every page provides a keyboard-focusable Skip to main content link (#main-content), logical Tab / Shift+Tab traversal order, visible focus outlines, Escape key dismissal on navigation drawers, and focusable horizontal table regions (tabindex="0" role="region").

Control 02

Semantic Landmarks & Screen Reader Support

Pages enforce strict heading hierarchies (<h1>–<h3>), ARIA landmarks (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <footer>), explicit table column scopes (scope="col"), and programmatic form field labels (<label for="...">) compatible with NVDA, VoiceOver, and TalkBack.

Control 03

Contrast Ratios, Minimum 12px Type & 200% Zoom

Self-hosted Inter and JetBrains Mono fonts enforce a strict 12px minimum floor across the entire repository, high-contrast foreground/background pairings meeting WCAG AA ratios, and responsive fluid layouts supporting up to 200% browser zoom without horizontal text clipping.

Control 04

Reduced Motion & Touch-Target Ergonomics

Animations respect operating-system prefers-reduced-motion: reduce preferences, decorative vector icons carry aria-hidden="true", and interactive navigation controls maintain generous touch-target hit areas across mobile and desktop viewports.

02. Statutory Safe Harbor & Regulatory Disclosures (Regulations 35A, 35B & 35E)

ZTDS.ai is maintained as an open technical specification portal sponsored by BrandMeWeb (Sole Proprietorship owned and operated by Ilya Sibiryakov). In accordance with international accessibility norms (EN 301 549, ADA Title III technical guidance) and the Israel Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Regulations (Service Accessibility Adjustments), 5773-2013, the following statutory provisions govern this portal:

1. Mandatory Prior Written Notice & 60-Day Statutory Cure Period (Regulation 35A)

Despite continuous automated CI/CD verification across HTML5 landmarks, contrast tokens, and keyboard focus states, isolated rendering variations may occasionally arise in complex code blocks, mathematical RFC tables, or browser extensions. Pursuant to Regulation 35A of the Israeli Service Accessibility Regulations and good-faith pre-litigation notice doctrine, any user encountering an accessibility barrier is requested to submit written notice specifying the affected page URL, browser/assistive technology combination, and nature of the issue so that our engineering team may remediate the defect within the statutory 60-day cure period prior to the initiation of any administrative or judicial proceeding.

2. Third-Party Repositories & External Scholarly Archives (Regulation 35B)

Pursuant to Regulation 35B, external platforms linked from ZTDS.ai — including GitHub source repositories, Zenodo / OSF / SSRN academic preprint archives, IETF Datatracker mirrors, npm / PyPI package registries, and external AI answer engines — operate outside the technical custody and DOM control of ZTDS.ai and remain subject to the respective accessibility architectures of their independent operators.

3. Sole-Proprietorship Statutory Exemption Reservation (Regulation 35E)

All WCAG 2.2 Level AA and IS 5568 engineering enhancements on ZTDS.ai are implemented proactively and in good faith as an open-standards public benefit. Operating as an individual sole proprietorship (BrandMeWeb, owned by Ilya Sibiryakov), the operator expressly reserves all statutory exemptions and defenses available to small-turnover and individual operators under Regulation 35E of the Service Accessibility Regulations without waiver of rights.
